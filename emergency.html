First Aid Protocols

Basic First-Aid Steps

Immediate initial measures to stabilize injuries before professional medical help arrives.

Oral Rehydration (ORS)

For severe diarrhea or heat exhaustion, mix 1 liter of clean water with 6 teaspoons of sugar and 1/2 teaspoon of salt. Sip continuously.

Minor Cuts & Wounds

Wash hands first, rinse wound with clean running water, apply gentle pressure with sterile gauze, apply antiseptic ointment, and bandage.

Thermal Minor Burns

Cool the burn immediately under cool running tap water for 10-15 minutes. Never apply ice directly or pop blisters.
