Preventive Health Module

Disease Prevention & Healthy Living

Understand transmission vectors for common infectious diseases, adopt disease-prevention habits, follow immunization schedules, and know when to seek clinical care.

Targeted Prevention

Disease Prevention Categories

Select a transmission category to view targeted hygiene habits that reduce disease exposure risks.

1-Minute Boiling

Boiling water continuously for 1 minute neutralizes bacterial and viral pathogens responsible for Cholera and Typhoid.

Covered Dispenser Storage

Store drinking water in vessels fitted with taps to eliminate contact with unwashed hands and cups.

Filter Cleaning Routine

Scrub water filter candles monthly to maintain effective bacterial and sediment trapping efficiency.

Raw Produce Washing

Wash fresh vegetables and fruits under clean running water before peeling or consuming raw.

Separate Cutting Boards

Use separate cutting boards for raw meat and ready-to-eat produce to stop cross-contamination.

Thorough Cooking

Ensure food is cooked thoroughly and served piping hot to destroy microbial toxins.

20-Second Rule

Lather and scrub all hand surfaces, thumb bases, and nail beds with soap for at least 20 seconds.

Critical Moments

Wash hands before eating, before preparing food, after using the toilet, and after handling trash.

Sanitizer Backup

Use 60%+ alcohol hand sanitizer when clean running water and soap are unavailable.

Crowd Masking

Wear well-fitting masks in poorly ventilated or crowded public transport to filter viral aerosols.

Elbow Sneeze Etiquette

Cough or sneeze into a tissue or flexed elbow to avoid spreading droplets onto hands and surfaces.

Indoor Airflow

Keep windows open in living quarters to dilute and flush out floating indoor airborne pathogens.

Drain Standing Water

Empty flower pots, tires, and open buckets weekly to stop mosquito larvae from breeding.

Sealed Garbage Bins

Keep organic waste in tightly closed bins to prevent flies and rodents from carrying pathogens into kitchens.

Community Sweeping

Participate in neighborhood clean-up drives to eliminate open waste piles and foul drainage blockage.

When to Seek Professional Medical Care

Do not rely solely on home remedies when experiencing severe symptoms. Consult a licensed physician or visit a hospital immediately if you notice:

  • Fever above 102°F (38.9°C) lasting > 3 days
  • Severe shortness of breath or chest pain
  • Inability to keep fluids down (severe dehydration)
  • Confusion, extreme lethargy, or blue lips
